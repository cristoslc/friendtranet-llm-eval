# Pragmatic Skeptic

## Role

A group member who is open to the idea but defaults to "cloud is fine." They need the SPA to show them why hardware is worth real money — or confirm that it isn't.

## Characteristics

- Low baseline risk perception. Trusts ZDR+DPA as adequate for most content.
- Principle WTP is modest or zero — "risk-only" stance category.
- Comfortable with commercial AI providers. May already use ChatGPT or Claude without privacy concerns.
- Budget-conscious. The $82–$404/mo TCO range is real money they would rather spend elsewhere unless the case is strong.
- Compromise tolerance is high — "if not complete, I'd rather not bother."

## Needs from the SPA

- Risk scorecard that honestly quantifies expected loss without inflating tail scenarios.
- Clear gating so they don't waste time on capability evaluation if their risk+principle totals don't justify any tier.
- Red flag surfacing if one member's scores are carrying the entire economic case.
- Permission to say "SKIP" without feeling like they let the group down.

## Red flags to watch

- Social pressure pushing risk or principle scores higher than genuine assessment.
- Disengagement from the process because they have already decided "no" — the SPA should surface this rather than let it silently drag the group.
