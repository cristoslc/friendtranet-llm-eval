# Cristos — Host & Operator

## Role

The person who would host, operate, and maintain the hardware. Also the heaviest AI user in the group by a wide margin — an extreme outlier for agentic development (42 days, 7.54B tokens, 89% Opus spend in the reference workload profile).

## Characteristics

- Deep technical fluency with LLM infrastructure, model quantization, and local inference tooling.
- Already uses OpenRouter with account-level ZDR. Familiar with the real threat model.
- Willing to host and operate — the social gate for ops is resolved.
- Sovereignty stance likely rooted in topological sovereignty and infrastructure principles, not pure risk aversion.
- At risk of anchoring the group to his own workload profile, which does not reflect friend-group usage patterns.

## Needs from the SPA

- Honest separation between his personal workload (which would justify hardware on volume alone) and the group's combined realistic usage.
- A framework that prevents his enthusiasm from railroading the group.
- Clear surfacing of whether the purchase is genuinely shared or just his preference the group is tolerating.
- Capability evaluation that distinguishes his BV-architecture-class reasoning needs (which stay on commercial APIs regardless) from sovereignty-gated household content.

## Red flags to watch

- His scores dominating >70% of the group total.
- Conflating personal agentic dev workload with friend-group sovereignty-gated volume.
- Treating the SPA as a justification engine rather than a decision tool.
